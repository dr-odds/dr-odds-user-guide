# Help and troubleshooting

## I cannot see a menu or game

1. Check the active account and current club.
2. Open the club lobby; account menus and club menus are different.
3. Ask the club owner to confirm that your membership is active and your role can open the menu.
4. If you own the club, check the feature’s availability and menu visibility.
5. Reopen the page and select the same club again.

## I am not sure whether a change was saved

Read the success or error message on that page. If the button is disabled, check required fields and allowed values. Before leaving, verify the club name and current settings so you do not save a change to the wrong club.

## I cannot join a game

Check active membership, table capacity, selected format and the allowed amount. A private table may require approval. For Slot Machine, the owner must enable play and the prize fund must be available.

## My balance or transfer looks wrong

Make sure you are not confusing club currency with diamonds and that you are in the right club. Review Wallet records or the club report with the correct date range. If you do not recognize a transfer or selected the wrong account, ask the club owner or support for help. Do not post private account identifiers or sign-in codes in a public message.

## A page does not open or is incomplete

Check your connection, then reopen the page. If another player can use the same game, check the browser or device where your page is open. In Backgammon, the confirmed game result matters more than the 3D board view; if the 3D view cannot load, the regular controls and confirmed result may still be available.

## Contact support

Open **Support & Help** from the main menu. Describe the page or club, what you were trying to do and roughly when the issue happened. Do not send your password, verification code, sign-in cookies, another person’s account balance or a screenshot containing private email addresses or full names. Hide personal details before attaching an image.
