## A few useful distinctions

- **Diamonds** belong to your platform account. **Club currency** belongs to one club and uses the symbol chosen by that club. Check the currency and amount shown on the page before you purchase, transfer or join a game.
- An account does not automatically have admin rights. Your role and the club owner’s access settings determine which club menus you can open.
- Some options appear only when a feature is enabled or available for that club. This can affect games, music, appearance settings and other owner tools.
- Screenshots in this guide are examples of the interface. Names and amounts shown in them are sample values; they do not represent anyone’s live account or club.
- If an option is missing, check the active account and club first. Then ask the club owner or support whether your role can use that feature.
