# Club roles and access

Every member has a role in each club. The same account can have a different role in another club, so check the active club before changing anything.

## Owner, manager, custom roles and players

- The **Owner** is responsible for the club’s settings.
- **Managers and custom roles** can perform the tasks the owner has allowed for that role. The word “manager” by itself does not guarantee that every club menu is available.
- A **Player** can use the club’s games and general member menus when membership is active.
- An **Invited** member may not be able to play yet. Check the membership status.

A typical starting setup gives the owner and manager access to areas such as Organization, invitations, Exchange and the club inbox; a player usually sees the inbox and permitted Exchange options, but not Organization or invitations. The owner’s current permission settings are what matter.

## Roles

Open **Roles** to add a role or rename a custom one. You can move roles to arrange the organization. The Owner stays at the top; the Player role stays at the bottom; a new role is placed just above Player.

- Use a clear name that explains the job, such as “Tournament manager.”
- The system Player role is locked and cannot be removed or moved.
- Role order affects the club’s reporting structure. Read the page instructions before rearranging it.
- Before deleting a custom role, check whether members still have it and which role they should use instead. Read the confirmation prompt carefully.

## Menu permissions

The **Permissions** page provides two controls:

1. **Role access:** Select a role and turn on the club menus its members can open.
2. **Individual access:** Search for a member and select them. By default, that person inherits access from their role. Turning on a personal list fully replaces the role’s menu access; it does not automatically combine with it.

The menus available for control may include:

- Club organization
- Invite to Club
- Club Exchange / wallet
- Club Inbox

After changing a role, review the enabled-menu count and select **Save**. When editing a person, check their name and current role again. If someone cannot open a page, first see whether they use an individual list or inherit the role’s settings.

### Example

If one player needs permission to invite members, you do not have to give the entire Player role that permission. You can keep the role-level setting and grant that one member a separate list. Remember: once the individual list is active, the role’s menu permissions no longer add to it.

## Organization and join requests

Use **Organization** to find a member through search, role filters and result pages. Where the feature is available, review or change the member’s position in the reporting structure. New join requests appear in a separate view.

- Before approving a request, verify the account and club.
- Auto-approve affects future requests. Read the confirmation after changing it.
- Moving a member in the organization may affect which people they can transfer currency to.
- Use the status shown on the page when reviewing invited, suspended or blocked members.

## Exchange permissions

The club’s **Exchange** settings can limit currency transfers to people in the same branch or allow broader transfers across the organization. This is separate from the menu permission: a member may see Exchange but still be limited in whom they can send currency to.

Players should check the recipient, amount and currency before transferring. If the rules are unclear, ask the club owner instead of trying different amounts.

## Ban Player

**Ban Player** may be available to the owner or an authorized manager. Before saving a restriction, verify the person, game or scope and duration. A restriction can be temporary or remain until it is lifted. Review the resulting list to confirm the intended member is shown.
