# Wallet, shop and data

## Two kinds of balance

You may see both **diamonds** and **club currency** in Dr.odds, but they are different:

- **Diamonds** belong to your platform account. They are used for products that the platform shop prices in diamonds.
- **Club currency** belongs to one club. That club chooses its symbol and display. Review its balance and transaction history from inside that club.

Before continuing with a purchase or a game, check the currency symbol and the amount shown on that page.

## Platform shop and club shop

- The **platform shop** lists products available to your account. Read the product details, diamond price and any stock or eligibility limits before buying.
- A **club shop** appears inside a club and may offer items or access for that club. It may use the club’s own currency.
- Limited products can become unavailable when stock runs out. For a timed or recurring item, read the product description.

Typical purchase steps: open the product, read its options and price, choose a quantity, check which balance will be used, then confirm. After purchase, check **Wallet / Inventory** for items that can be kept or used. Do not confirm a purchase if the price or active account is unclear.

## Club wallet and Exchange

In the platform lobby, **Wallet** may open your inventory. Inside a club, **Wallet** or **Exchange** refers to that club’s currency activity.

Depending on your role and permissions, you may see:

- **Transfer:** Find an allowed recipient, enter an amount and confirm it. Check the recipient and currency again before the final step.
- **Records:** Review completed movements. Filter by date, direction or person when those controls are available to your role.
- **Rake-back:** Some clubs or games may show a separate view of eligible returns.

Transfer club currency only when you have permission and recognize the recipient. If you send it to the wrong person, the club owner may need to review it. Club owners decide whether members can transfer within their reporting branch or more broadly.

## Data, history and analysis

Use **Data** to review your matches and statistics. Page names and filters can vary by game. Open a match to review its result and recorded moves; if analysis is available, it can offer more detail.

- Check filters before drawing a conclusion; results may show only a date range, game type or club.
- Analysis is not available for every game or account.
- A move list may group play by game or set. Do not confuse the result on record with a move you are currently reviewing.
- If you see no matches, check the date range, game type and selected club.
